Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who We Are

AuditorA Home Inspection ("we," "our," or "us") is a licensed home inspection business operating in Greater Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, owned and operated by Vahid Alikhanzadeh, a home inspector licensed by Consumer Protection BC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website (auditora.ca) or our inspection services.

What Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you request a quote, submit our contact form, or communicate with us:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • The address and details of the property to be inspected
  • Property type, size, and timeline information you provide
  • Any messages, notes, or questions you send us

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Provide you with an accurate inspection quote
  • Schedule and confirm your home inspection
  • Deliver your inspection report and any follow-up communication
  • Respond to your questions or requests
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations in British Columbia

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not send marketing emails without your consent.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to safeguard your personal information. Our website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Access to your information is limited to what is necessary to provide our services to you.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services which may process data as part of their normal operation:

  • Netlify — website hosting and form submission handling
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

Your Rights Under BC PIPA

Under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent for certain uses of your information
  • Request deletion of your information where legally permissible

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at alikhanzadehvahid@yahoo.com.

Retention

We retain inspection records and related client information for as long as required by British Columbia regulations governing licensed home inspectors, and as reasonably necessary for business and legal purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
