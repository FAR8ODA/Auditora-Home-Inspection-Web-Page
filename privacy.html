Who we are

AuditorA Home Inspection is operated by Vahid Alikhanzadeh, a home inspector licensed by Consumer Protection BC under licence #89931, serving Greater Vancouver, British Columbia. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use auditora.ca or our inspection services.

Information we collect

When you request an inspection or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your full legal name, phone number and email address
  • The address and details of the property to be inspected
  • Property type, approximate size, year built and occupancy status
  • Strata name and unit number where applicable
  • Access instructions, parking details and site condition information
  • Realtor contact information, only where you provide and authorize it
  • Any messages, questions or comments you send us

How we use your information

Your information is used only to:

  • Confirm the inspection fee and availability for your property
  • Schedule, prepare for and carry out your inspection
  • Prepare and deliver your inspection report
  • Respond to your questions and provide follow-up support
  • Meet record-keeping obligations under British Columbia regulation

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing email without your consent.

How we protect your information

This website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Access to your information is limited to what is necessary to provide our services to you.

Service providers

This website uses third-party services that may process data as part of their normal operation, including our website host and web font provider. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Your rights under BC PIPA

Under British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent for certain uses of your information
  • Request deletion of your information where legally permissible

To exercise any of these rights, email info@auditora.ca.

Retention

Inspection records and related client information are retained as required by British Columbia regulation governing licensed home inspectors, and as reasonably necessary for business and legal purposes.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Call or text 604-719-5840 or email info@auditora.ca.
