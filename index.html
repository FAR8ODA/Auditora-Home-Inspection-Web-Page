How It Works

From Request to Report

A clear, straightforward process from your first message to your written report.

1

Request an inspection

Call, text or email, or send your property details through the request form.

2

Confirm the details

The appointment and final inspection fee are confirmed only after AuditorA reviews the property information.

3

Sign the agreement

A personalized HIABC inspection contract will be provided for review and electronic signature before the inspection. The inspection cannot proceed until the agreement has been accepted and signed.

4

Inspection and report

A clear digital inspection report is normally provided within 24 hours after the inspection.
