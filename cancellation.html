Cancellation and rescheduling

The inspection may be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours before the scheduled inspection time without charge. Cancellations or rescheduling requests received less than 24 hours before the scheduled inspection time are subject to a $100 cancellation fee.

We understand that property transactions involve a lot of moving parts and that plans change. Please let us know as early as you can so the appointment slot can be offered to someone else.

How to cancel or reschedule

Please contact Vahid directly as soon as you know:

  • Call or text 604-719-5840
  • Email alikhanzadehvahid@yahoo.com

Please include your name, the property address and the scheduled inspection date in your message.

Property access on the day

Please ensure the property is accessible at the confirmed time and that utilities are operating where possible. Review Prepare for Your Inspection before your appointment.

Areas that are unsafe, locked, obstructed or otherwise inaccessible may not be inspected and will be identified in the inspection report. A return visit, if requested and available, may require an additional fee.

Weather and safety

Some conditions, such as snow, ice or unsafe roof surfaces, may prevent parts of an inspection from being completed safely. In those cases the accessible areas are inspected and any limitations are noted in the report.

Payment

Payment is due at the time of the inspection. Available payment methods will be confirmed when your appointment is scheduled.

This cancellation policy applies to the appointment. The inspection itself is governed by the signed HIABC inspection contract. See Inspection Contract for details.
