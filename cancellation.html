Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We understand that buying or selling a home involves a lot of moving parts and things change. We aim to be as flexible as reasonably possible, but we do ask for fair notice when plans change.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

✅ 48+ Hours Notice — No Charge

Cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours before your scheduled inspection and there is no cancellation fee. We'll reschedule at no extra cost, or issue a full refund if you prefer.

⚠️ 24–48 Hours Notice — 50% Fee

Cancellations or reschedules made between 24 and 48 hours before the inspection are subject to a fee of 50% of the quoted inspection price. The remaining balance can be applied toward a rescheduled inspection.

❌ Under 24 Hours or No Access — Full Fee

Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice, or failure to provide property access at the scheduled time, are subject to the full quoted inspection fee. This covers reserved time, travel, and preparation.

How to Cancel or Reschedule

Please contact us as early as possible:

  • Call or text 604-719-5840
  • Email alikhanzadehvahid@yahoo.com

Include your name, property address, and scheduled inspection date in your message.

Property Access

Please ensure the property is accessible at the scheduled time and that utilities (power, water, gas) are turned on. If the inspector arrives and cannot gain access or complete the inspection due to conditions outside our control, the full fee may apply.

Weather & Safety

Certain conditions — such as heavy snow, ice, or unsafe roof conditions — may prevent portions of an inspection from being safely completed. In these cases we will inspect everything safely accessible and clearly note any limitations in your report. If a reschedule is needed due to weather, no cancellation fee applies.

Special Circumstances

Emergencies happen. If you need to cancel due to a genuine emergency, please reach out and we will work with you. We're a local business and we treat people like people.

Deal Fell Through?

If your real estate transaction falls through after your inspection is scheduled, contact us right away. Depending on timing, we may be able to reschedule or apply a credit toward a future inspection.

Need to Change Your Booking?

Reach out as early as you can and we'll sort it out.
