My Story

Hi, I'm Vahid Alikhanzadeh

I started AuditorA Home Inspection with one goal: to give people in Greater Vancouver an inspector they can genuinely trust. Someone who shows up prepared, explains things clearly, and puts your interests ahead of a fast turnaround.

I'm licensed by Consumer Protection BC and a member of the Home Inspectors Association of British Columbia (HIABC) — meaning every inspection I perform follows established professional standards, not shortcuts.

I've invested in professional-grade equipment including thermal imaging and moisture detection tools, because a real inspection goes well beyond what the eye alone can see. Hidden moisture behind a wall or an overheating electrical connection won't show up in a casual walkthrough — but it will show up in mine.

"A home is one of the biggest investments of your life. You deserve honest, detailed information — not a rushed walkthrough."

Every inspection follows the same approach: methodical, thorough, and clearly communicated before, during, and after. You're always welcome to attend and ask me anything as we go — most clients find that's where they learn the most about their property.

I serve clients throughout Vancouver, Burnaby, North Vancouver, Richmond, Coquitlam, Surrey, and the wider Lower Mainland. I built AuditorA to be the kind of company I'd want to hire myself.
