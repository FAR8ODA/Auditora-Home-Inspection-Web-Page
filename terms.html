Agreement to Terms

By requesting or booking an inspection with AuditorA Home Inspection ("AuditorA"), you agree to the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully.

Licensing

AuditorA Home Inspection is operated by Vahid Alikhanzadeh, a home inspector licensed by Consumer Protection BC and a member of the Home Inspectors Association of British Columbia (HIABC). All inspections are performed in accordance with applicable BC licensing requirements and HIABC standards of practice.

Scope of Inspection

Our inspections are visual, non-invasive examinations of the readily accessible areas and components of a residential property. We inspect the following systems where accessible:

  • Structure and foundation (visible areas)
  • Roof, attic, and insulation
  • Electrical systems
  • Plumbing systems
  • Heating, ventilation, and cooling (HVAC)
  • Exterior envelope and interior components
  • Windows, doors, and finishes

What Is Not Included

A home inspection is not a warranty, guarantee, or insurance policy. We do not:

  • Perform destructive or invasive testing
  • Move furniture, stored items, or personal belongings
  • Inspect concealed, inaccessible, or unsafe areas
  • Provide engineering, environmental, or code-compliance analysis
  • Test for mould, asbestos, radon, or other environmental hazards unless separately arranged
  • Predict future performance or remaining life of any component

Quotes and Pricing

Quotes are provided based on the property information you supply. If the property differs materially from what was described — for example, significantly larger square footage or additional structures — we will discuss any adjustment with you before proceeding. You will never be charged more than quoted without your agreement.

Report Delivery

Your written inspection report is typically delivered electronically within 24 hours of the completed inspection. The report reflects observable conditions at the time of inspection only.

Limitation of Liability

The total liability of AuditorA Home Inspection for any claim arising out of or related to an inspection shall not exceed the fee paid for that inspection. We are not responsible for conditions that were not visible, accessible, or reasonably detectable using standard non-invasive inspection methods at the time of inspection.

Client Attendance

You are welcome and encouraged to attend your inspection. Attending allows you to ask questions and better understand the findings. Please arrive on time, as the inspection will begin at the scheduled time.

Property Access

It is the client's responsibility to ensure the inspector has safe and full access to the property at the scheduled time, including utilities being turned on. If access cannot be provided, cancellation terms may apply.
