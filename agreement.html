The inspection agreement

A personalized HIABC inspection contract will be provided for review and electronic signature before the inspection. The inspection cannot proceed until the agreement has been accepted and signed.

The contract sets out what is included in the inspection, what falls outside it, and the responsibilities of both the client and the inspector. Please read it carefully and ask any questions before signing.

Scope of the inspection

The inspection is conducted in accordance with the HIABC Scope of Inspection.

A visual inspection of readily accessible systems and components within the HIABC Scope of Inspection. The inspection does not include destructive or invasive testing, and areas that are concealed, locked, obstructed or unsafe to access cannot be inspected.

Important. The Website Terms published on this site are for use of the website only. They do not replace, modify or override the signed HIABC inspection contract, which governs the inspection itself.

Condo inspections

A condo inspection primarily covers the readily accessible systems and components within the unit and any other areas specifically included and accessible at the time of inspection. Clients should separately review strata documents, depreciation reports, meeting minutes and planned expenditures.

Before your appointment

Please review Prepare for Your Inspection so the property is ready and as much of it as possible can be inspected on the day.

Payment

Payment is due at the time of the inspection. Available payment methods will be confirmed when your appointment is scheduled.

Cancellation

The inspection may be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours before the scheduled inspection time without charge. Cancellations or rescheduling requests received less than 24 hours before the scheduled inspection time are subject to a $100 cancellation fee.

See the cancellation policy for full details.

Your report

A clear digital inspection report is normally provided within 24 hours after the inspection.
