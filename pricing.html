Common Questions

Quote & Booking FAQ

How quickly will I get my quote? +
If you call, we can usually give you a quote right on the phone. Email inquiries typically get a response within a few hours during business hours.
What info do you need for a quote? +
Property address, approximate square footage, property type (house, condo, townhouse), and roughly when you need the inspection done. If you don't have all of it, that's fine — just reach out and we'll figure it out.
Is the quote binding? +
The quoted price is what you pay. If something significantly different is discovered on arrival — like the property being much larger than described — we'll discuss it with you before proceeding. No surprise charges.
Is thermal imaging really included? +
Yes, on every inspection at no extra charge. Thermal imaging helps detect hidden moisture, insulation gaps, and electrical hotspots that a visual inspection alone would miss.
How long does an inspection take? +
Most inspections take 2–3.5 hours depending on size and complexity. We never rush. You're welcome to attend and ask questions throughout.
When do I receive my report? +
Your full digital report is typically delivered within 24 hours, including photos, findings, and clear explanations of everything we found.
What if I need to cancel? +
We ask for at least 48 hours notice for cancellations or reschedules. See our full cancellation policy for details.
